# Gamme: product one-pager

**What it is:** Learn the patterns that beat games, then drill them

**Who it is for:** Someone who picks up patterns fast and resents being taught
slowly. They do not want a tutorial that walks them through a first game. They
want the compressed rule set, the named patterns that fall out of it, and a way
to find out in ninety seconds whether they actually have it.

**Register:** product (a tool someone uses), not brand (a page someone visits).

**The scene:** A laptop, one browser tab, fifteen minutes between other things.
They open a pattern card, read four lines, hit Drill, get six positions wrong,
and go back to the card knowing exactly which one they misread. Dark room,
dense screen, no scrolling for the point.

**What done looks like:** They can name the pattern in front of them and say
what it forces, without counting from scratch. And for Minesweeper specifically:
they know which rule the site they are playing on actually uses, so a board that
cannot be solved by logic no longer feels like their failure.
